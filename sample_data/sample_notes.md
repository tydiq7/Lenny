# Supply and Demand

**Law of demand:** As the price of a good rises, the quantity demanded falls, all else equal. The demand curve slopes downward.

**Law of supply:** As the price of a good rises, the quantity supplied rises, all else equal. The supply curve slopes upward.

**Equilibrium:** The market equilibrium is the price where quantity supplied equals quantity demanded. If price is above equilibrium there is a surplus, so prices fall. If price is below equilibrium there is a shortage, so prices rise.

**Price elasticity of demand:** Measures the percentage change in quantity demanded divided by the percentage change in price. Demand is elastic if the absolute value is greater than 1, and inelastic if it is less than 1. Necessities like insulin tend to be inelastic; luxuries tend to be elastic.

**Shifts vs. movements:** A change in the good's own price moves along the curve. A change in income, tastes, or the price of related goods shifts the whole demand curve. A change in input costs or technology shifts the supply curve.
